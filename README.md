# Awesome Web Games 🎮

A curated list of awesome web games you can play **instantly in your browser** — no downloads, no installs. Every entry is hand-checked and playable right now.

★ marks a game made by the maintainer of this list.

## Puzzle

- [2048](https://play2048.co) - The classic sliding-tile number puzzle; join tiles to reach 2048.
- [0h n0](https://0hn0.com) - A minimalist binary logic puzzle: fill the grid with red and blue tiles under three simple rules.
- [The Password Game](https://neal.fun/password-game/) - A puzzle game about creating a password, with rules that escalate into delightful absurdity.
- [Infinite Craft](https://infinite-craft.com/) - An endless crafting sandbox where you combine elements to discover new ones.
- [Little Alchemy 2](https://littlealchemy2.com/) - Combine base elements to discover hundreds of items in this charming crafting puzzle.
- [Web Sudoku](https://www.websudoku.com/) - The long-running home of online Sudoku, with difficulties from easy to evil.
- [Gridland](https://gridland.doublespeakgames.com/) - A match-3 puzzle fused with survival: build by day, defend by night.

## Daily

- [Wordle](https://www.nytimes.com/games/wordle/index.html) - Guess the five-letter word in six tries; the daily word game that started it all.
- [Play Balance Grid](https://playbalancegrid.com) ★ - A daily 6x6 binary logic puzzle in English and German: balance each row and column with 0s and 1s.
- [Connections](https://www.nytimes.com/games/connections) - Group 16 words into four related categories in this daily NYT puzzle.
- [Globle](https://globle-game.com) - A daily geography guessing game: find the mystery country with proximity hints.
- [Worldle](https://worldle.teuteuf.fr) - Guess the country from its silhouette in six tries, with distance and direction clues.
- [Framed](https://framed.wtf) - Guess the movie from a single frame, with new stills revealed after each miss.
- [Redactle](https://redactle.net) - Guess a Wikipedia article from its fully redacted text; each solved word unmasks the page.

## Arcade

- [Celeste Classic](https://mattmakesgames.itch.io/celesteclassic) - The original PICO-8 prototype of Celeste: a tight, challenging precision platformer.
- [Chrome T-Rex](https://elgoog.im/t-rex/) - The offline dinosaur endless runner, playable in your browser anytime.
- [Google Snake](https://www.google.com/fbx?fbx=snake_arcade) - Google's polished take on the classic Snake, with multiple modes and settings.
- [Subway Surfers](https://poki.com/en/g/subway-surfers) - The iconic endless runner: dodge trains and collect coins.
- [Run 3](https://player03.com/) - Run and jump through tunnels in space in this beloved endless runner.
- [Stickman Hook](https://poki.com/en/g/stickman-hook) - Swing a stickman through physics-based levels with a single tap.

## Idle / Incremental

- [Cookie Clicker](https://orteil.dashnet.org/cookieclicker/) - The idle game that defined the genre: bake cookies, buy upgrades, automate everything.
- [A Dark Room](https://doublespeakgames.github.io/adarkroom/) - Starts as a minimal clicker in a dark room and unfolds into a strange RPG adventure.
- [Candy Box 2](https://candybox2.github.io/) - An ASCII-art incremental RPG packed with quests, humor, and secrets.
- [Universal Paperclips](https://www.decisionproblem.com/paperclips/) - An AI paperclip factory simulator; a cult classic of the incremental genre.
- [Trimps](https://trimps.github.io/) - A deep incremental about leading tiny creatures through zones and challenges.
- [Kittens Game](https://kittensgame.com/) - A village-management incremental with surprising depth, from the stone age to space.
- [Antimatter Dimensions](https://ivark.github.io/) - A prestige-layer-heavy incremental about producing antimatter across dimensions.

## Multiplayer

- [Agar.io](https://agar.io/) - Eat cells and outgrow rivals in the .io game that launched a thousand clones.
- [Slither.io](https://slither.io/) - Snake meets MMO: outmaneuver other players to become the longest snake.
- [Diep.io](https://diep.io/) - Tank combat in a 2D arena with deep upgrade trees; solo or team modes.
- [Tetr.io](https://tetr.io/) - Modern competitive Tetris with ranked matchmaking, played entirely in the browser.
- [Skribbl.io](https://skribbl.io/) - Draw and guess with friends or strangers in this multiplayer drawing party game.
- [Gartic Phone](https://garticphone.com/) - The telephone game meets drawing: hilarious multiplayer rounds with friends.
- [Lichess](https://lichess.org/) - Free, open-source chess with instant casual matchmaking; no account required.

## Word & Card

- [Set with Friends](https://setwithfriends.com/) - The classic pattern-recognition card game Set, playable live with friends.
- [Codenames](https://codenames.game/) - An online version of the hit word-association party game.
- [World of Solitaire](https://worldofsolitaire.com/) - Over 50 solitaire variants in a clean, no-frills interface.
- [Lexulous](https://www.lexulous.com/) - Scrabble-style word battles against friends or the computer; guest play supported.
- [Semantle](https://semantle.com/) - Guess the secret word using semantic similarity hints.
- [The Wiki Game](https://www.thewikigame.com/) - Race from one Wikipedia article to another using only links; daily challenges included.
